Offer Top 6 Playoff for both standard knockouts and the two breakout knockouts.

**Why:** The user supplied a Top 6 diagram and explicitly chose “Both standard and breakout knockouts.”

**How to apply:** Each participating bracket needs six qualifiers under the existing qualification rules. Seeds 1 and 2 receive semifinal byes; the quarterfinals are 3 versus 6 and 4 versus 5. The proposed standard semifinal pairing is 1 versus the winner of 4/5, and 2 versus the winner of 3/6. Do not advance extra teams or silently truncate qualifiers to make the count fit.
