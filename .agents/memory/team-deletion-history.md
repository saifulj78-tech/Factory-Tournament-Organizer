---
name: Team deletion and match history
description: Why team removal must preserve fixtures and historical results
---

Team removal offers two explicit choices: replace the team in fixtures using another same-group team (clearing affected results), or remove it without replacement (removing its group fixtures and reconciling the knockout bracket). Never leave fixtures referencing a deleted team.

**Why:** Match cards need a live team record for names. The organizer wants both a replacement option and deletion without replacement, with fixture adjustments and notices. Silently inheriting old results or erasing unrelated knockout scores would corrupt standings and history.

**How to apply:** Require organizer confirmation showing fixture and score impact. Preserve unrelated results, avoid self-matches and duplicate knockout entrants, and inform connected organizers. If a bracket path cannot be safely recalculated, refuse the change rather than silently corrupting it.
