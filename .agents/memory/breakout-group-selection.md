name: Breakout group selection
description: Why the two breakout knockouts are selected by source groups rather than standings tiers
---

The two breakout knockouts are assigned manually by source group, not by top-versus-bottom finishing positions.

**Why:** The user clarified: “It will depend on which group there are from. Selected groups for each knockout.”

**How to apply:** Keep group selection under organizer control. Do not reinterpret bracket names such as Gold and Silver as automatic standings-based qualification tiers.

Group selection only decides the destination bracket. It must not replace the previous qualification and seeding criteria or automatically advance every team in a selected group.

**Why:** The user corrected the initial all-teams implementation: “There should still be seeding of who proceeds to knockout according to precious criterias.”

**How to apply:** Apply the existing qualifiers-per-group, tiebreakers, wildcard selection and manual seeding rules first; then route those qualifiers according to their source groups.

Breakout mode must retain the green automatic-qualifier indicators in group standings, for organizers as well as spectators.

**Why:** The user reported the missing green indicators after breakout qualification was restored.

**How to apply:** Treat qualification visibility as shared behavior across both standings views, not as a feature limited to the old single-bracket tournament format.
